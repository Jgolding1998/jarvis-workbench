PLAIN-LANGUAGE PRIVACY NOTES

Your setup stays yours.

This website

The setup guide asks about uses, budget, equipment, and tool preferences. It does not ask for your name, email, account password, or API key. It does not request a camera or microphone and includes no analytics or advertising scripts. Browser downloads happen only when you choose them.

Any saved setup preferences belong in your browser or a plan file you download. Do not add secrets to a setup plan. The hosting provider may receive normal connection information when serving the website.

The desktop application

The desktop program is separate from this website. Optional hand and presence features process camera frames on the computer. Optional offline command recognition uses a local language model. Device access and desktop actions require local permission.

Optional service guidance

Links to tool providers take you to their websites, which have their own terms and privacy policies. The setup guide does not provision accounts, make purchases, or connect Jarvis to a paid AI or voice provider.

Before sharing

Review plan files and screenshots before sharing them. The original PDF renamer keeps the contents and metadata of renamed documents unchanged. Never upload private documents, camera images, traffic captures, or credentials to a public issue.
