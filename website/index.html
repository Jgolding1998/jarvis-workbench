HAND CONTROL IN FOUR MOVES

Say it. Show it.
Point. Clap to stop.

This is the whole vocabulary. You can learn it in a minute and practice it below without a camera.

  1. 01

    Say “watch me”

    Or type it. Jarvis turns on the camera for hand control only. “Start hand tracking” works too.

  2. 02

    Show an open hand

    Palm toward the camera, about an arm’s length away, with light in front of you. Jarvis finds 21 points on your hand.

  3. 03

    Point, then pinch

    Your index fingertip moves the pointer. Thumb to middle finger is a left click. Thumb to ring finger is a right click. Holding a pinch clicks once.

  4. 04

    Clap to stop

    Hold both hands apart, then bring them together. The camera sees the clap. No microphone is used.

Esc

Esc always stops hand control. It works on its own, even if the camera is slow.

◉

A clap stops hand control only. The presence guard has its own switch and stays as you left it.

WHAT YOUR COMPUTER NEEDS

Four pieces.
In plain words.

Hand control is built from a few free parts. The setup guide checks each one and shows what is missing, so nothing is left as a guess. If a piece is missing, hand control stays off and you can still use the practice pad.

Check my computer’s list ↗
  1. 4

    A quick camera check

    Sit about an arm’s length away. Face the light rather than a window. Show an open hand and practice before you let it move the real cursor.

  2. 3

    The hand model

    A small file that knows what a hand looks like. You download it once, as its own step. Jarvis checks it is the expected file, and it runs on your computer.

  3. 2

    Optional add-on packages

    Free add-ons for the features you pick: a camera reader and a hand finder for hand control, a listener for offline voice. Skip a feature and you skip its add-ons.

  4. 1

    Python

    The free engine Jarvis runs on. Version 3.11 or newer. If it is missing, the launcher says so and tells you where to get it.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few straight answers.

Does this page install anything?

No. It helps you choose a setup and download a plan. The desktop package runs separately on your computer. Its launcher needs Python 3.11 or later and tells you where to get it if it is missing.

Does my camera footage leave the computer?

The desktop hand and presence adapters process frames locally. This website never asks for camera access, and the practice pad does not use a camera. Model and package downloads are separate setup steps.

What if I clap while the presence guard is on?

Only hand control stops. The presence guard is armed and disarmed with its own switch, so a clap never turns it off.

Can I connect the AI tools I already pay for?

The guide records the tools you already use and explains current setup requirements. Cloud AI and paid voice entries are planning guidance, not implemented Jarvis connectors. It never asks you to paste an API key here.

Is it ready to control my computer immediately?

Start with the practice pad. Install and check the optional pieces, do the camera check, then explicitly enable live actions on your computer. Camera accuracy and the physical stops need testing on your own hardware.
